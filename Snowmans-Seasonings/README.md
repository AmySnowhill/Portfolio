## 🔑 Key Highlights  

- Five proprietary spice blends: **Blackening Spice, Spicy Sugar, Jerk Seasoning, Curry Spice, Season Salt**  
- Pricing: **$4.00 per ounce**  
- Target audience: **Gen Z & Millennial home cooks (ages 18–40)**  
- Marketing channels: **TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, blog, local radio**  
- Growth plan: **expand product line, reach $50K in sales, enter national retail distribution**
